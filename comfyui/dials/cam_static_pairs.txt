# cam_static dial (ComfyUI-Dials DialsMakeDial, post_refiner hook, FL2VA int8). A (-) = camera on a tripod, B (+) = camera pushes in. Subjects: A bartender in a white shirt / An old man in a brown cardigan / A young woman in a green dress / A pianist in a dark blue suit. Use at -2 together with pi-Flow max_raw_t 1.0.
{subject} works at a counter in a small cafe. The camera is mounted on a tripod and stays perfectly still for the whole shot; the framing never changes. ||| {subject} works at a counter in a small cafe. The camera slowly pushes in toward {subject}, the framing getting tighter and tighter through the shot.
{subject} reads a book in a quiet library. Locked-off static shot: no zoom, no pan, no dolly, the frame is fixed from the first frame to the last. ||| {subject} reads a book in a quiet library. Slow dolly-in shot: the camera moves steadily closer, zooming in until {subject} fills the frame.
{subject} plays a piano in a hotel lounge. A fixed camera holds the same wide framing for the entire clip, nothing in the frame moves except the people. ||| {subject} plays a piano in a hotel lounge. The camera creeps forward and zooms in, ending on a close-up of {subject}'s hands and face.
{subject} polishes glasses behind a bar. Static camera, steady wide shot, the whole room stays in frame at the same size all the time. ||| {subject} polishes glasses behind a bar. Push-in shot: the camera drifts toward {subject}, the room growing larger and leaving the frame edges.
{subject} waters plants on a balcony. The shot is a still frame from a camera bolted down, the composition identical at the start and at the end. ||| {subject} waters plants on a balcony. The camera zooms in continuously, so the end of the clip is a much tighter shot than the start.
{subject} sorts papers at a desk in an office. Tripod shot, completely motionless camera, constant field of view. ||| {subject} sorts papers at a desk in an office. Slow zoom-in, the field of view narrowing steadily over the clip.
